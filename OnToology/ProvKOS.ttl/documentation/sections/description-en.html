ProvKOS: An Application Profile for Documenting Provenance in Knowledge Organization Systems: Description back to ToC

ProvKOS extends SKOS and PROV-O to represent the provenance of editorial activities in Knowledge Organization Systems (KOS): typed change activities, warrant structures, and the relationships among entities, activities, agents, and warrants. Revised in response to peer review (2026): class renamed to ProvKOS:ClassNumber; domain of ProvKOS:hasLabel broadened to prov:Entity; explicit PROV-O sub-property alignments; example individuals moved to separate data files.
